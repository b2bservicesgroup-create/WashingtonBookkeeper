If your business files a Washington excise tax return, a change is coming with your first 2027 filing. The Washington State Department of Revenue (DOR) has announced that, starting **January 1, 2027**, the Business & Occupation (B&O) tax rate for several common classifications rises to a flat **0.5%**.

The increase looks small on paper, a fraction of a percent. But B&O is a tax on **gross receipts**, not profit, so it applies to every dollar that comes in, whether or not you made money on it. Here's what's changing and what to do about it.

## Which B&O classifications are increasing?

| B&O classification | Rate through 12/31/2026 | Rate starting 1/1/2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Retailing | 0.471% | 0.5% |
| Wholesaling | 0.484% | 0.5% |
| Manufacturing | 0.484% | 0.5% |
| Extracting | 0.484% | 0.5% |
| Processing for Hire; Printing & Publishing (non-newspaper) | 0.484% | 0.5% |
| Insurance Producers; Surplus Line Brokers; Child Care | 0.484% | 0.5% |
| Warehousing; Public Road Construction; Government Contracting; Chemical Dependency Centers | 0.484% | 0.5% |

## What does this mean in real dollars?

A few quick examples (B&O only, before any credits):

- **A retailer with $500,000 in gross sales:** $2,355 in B&O at the current rate vs. **$2,500** in 2027, an increase of **$145** per year.
- **A wholesaler with $1,000,000 in gross sales:** $4,840 vs. **$5,000**, an increase of **$160** per year.
- **A manufacturer with $2,500,000 in gross receipts:** $12,100 vs. **$12,500**, an increase of **$400** per year.

These aren't huge numbers for most small businesses, but they add up, especially if you report under more than one classification or your margins are thin. If you're eligible for the Small Business B&O Tax Credit, your credit may also shift as your tax goes up, so it's worth having your bookkeeper take a look.

## What you need to do on your excise tax return

DOR notes that some of the old combined classifications **won't be available anymore** on 2027 returns. If you used one of them, you may need to add the new classification yourself:

1. Log in to My DOR and open your excise tax return.
2. Select the **"Add/Delete Tax classifications"** button.
3. Add the new classification(s) that match your business activity.
4. Double-check that your reported income lands on the correct line before you submit.

If you file monthly, this applies to your **January 2027** return (due in February). Quarterly filers will see it on their **Q1 2027** return, and annual filers on their 2027 annual return.

## Why is this happening?

The changes come from two pieces of legislation passed by the Washington Legislature: **ESSB 2081** (Chapter 240, Laws of 2025) and **ESSB 6228** (Chapter 240, Laws of 2026). You can read DOR's full notice, including a few specialized industries not listed here, on the [Department of Revenue website](https://dor.wa.gov/forms-publications/publications-subject/special-notices/bo-tax-rate-increases-05-certain-activities-and-new-line-codes).

## Quick checklist before January

- Confirm which B&O classification(s) your business reports under.
- Update your 2027 budget and pricing for the higher rate.
- If you use a combined line code that's being split, know which new line you'll report on.
- Make sure whoever files your excise tax return knows about the change.
